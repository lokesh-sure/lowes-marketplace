Image ratio guide

These are recommended upload sizes based on the homepage display frames.

Store logo · 3:1

Example: 1200 × 400 px. The header keeps the full image visible; transparent PNG is recommended.

Product image · 1:1

Example: 1000 × 1000 px. Keep the product centered; product cards crop to fit their image area.

Category image · 4:3

Example: 1200 × 900 px. Category tiles fill their frame and crop the edges, so center the subject.

Sale poster · 3:1

Example: 1500 × 500 px. The homepage fits the whole image in a matching banner frame.
