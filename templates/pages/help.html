Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Select a product, add it to your cart, proceed to checkout, and place your order.

How can I cancel an order?

Go to My Orders and choose Cancel Order if it is eligible.

How do I track my order?

You can track your order from the My Orders page after logging in.

What payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery, UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, and Net Banking.
